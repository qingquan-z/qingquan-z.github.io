The Special Session on Games at IEEE CEC 2027 brings together researchers and practitioners working on computational intelligence for games. Topics include evolutionary computation, learning to play games, procedural content generation, and large language models in games.

IEEE CEC 2027 takes place in Edinburgh, Scotland, UK, on 25–28 July 2027. The session website provides the scope, organisers, important dates, and author instructions.
